## DELIVERY RULES

- Pause 0.5s before the words **"scenario rail"** and **"hands-free"**.
- Drop volume slightly on the twist section — quiet reads as authority.
- Never say: "basically", "actually", "so".
- Rehearse the demo section until mouse movement and words are perfectly in sync —
  that sync is what makes judges feel it's real.
- Practice 5 full run-throughs with a timer before the final record.
